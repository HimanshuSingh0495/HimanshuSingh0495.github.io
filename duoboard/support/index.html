Support

Email duoapps.support+duoboard@gmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two working days. Include your iPhone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Can my client see my costs?
No. The client's side only ever receives client prices. Costs and margins appear only on your side.
How do I share a proposal?
Open the proposal and choose Share PDF.
