Support

Email duoapps.support+duonight@gmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two working days. Include your iPhone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

How do we use tent mode?
Open Settings and choose Tent. The far half of the screen turns to face the other person.
How do we clear our history?
Delete the app. All of its data is on your phone and goes with it.
