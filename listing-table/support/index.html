Email duoapps.support+listing-table@gmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two working days. Include your iPhone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

The seller's side is upside down
That's tent mode. If you're presenting with the phone flat, choose Flat in Settings.
Can I leave a PDF instead of a link?
Yes. In the leave-behind, choose Share PDF instead of Create Share Link.
