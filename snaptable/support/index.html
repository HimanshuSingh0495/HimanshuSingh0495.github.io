Support

Email duoapps.support+snaptable@gmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two working days. Include your iPhone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Can we play on two phones?
Not yet. SnapTable is designed for one phone shared across a table.
Where did my decks go?
Decks are stored on the phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
