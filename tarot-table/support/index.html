Support

Email duoapps.support+tarot-table@gmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two working days. Include your iPhone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

My clients don't appear on my iPad
Check that iCloud sync is on in the app's Settings and that both devices use the same Apple Account.
How do I delete a client?
Open the client and choose Delete client. If iCloud sync is on, it's removed from your other devices too.
